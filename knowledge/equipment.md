# Equipment Manual

## Irrigation Pump Model AP-2000
- Operating pressure: 40-60 PSI
- Flow rate: 150L/min
- Service interval: Every 500 hours

## Soil Moisture Sensors
- Calibrate every 6 months
- Replace batteries annually
- Optimal reading range: 30-70%

## Control Units
- Firmware updates: Check quarterly
- Backup settings before any update
