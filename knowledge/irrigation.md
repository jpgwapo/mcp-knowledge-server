# Irrigation System Guide

## Common Issues
- Blocked drip lines: Clean filters every 3 months
- Low pressure: Check main valve and pump
- Uneven watering: Inspect emitters for blockages

## Maintenance Schedule
- Weekly: Visual inspection of all zones
- Monthly: Filter cleaning
- Quarterly: Full pressure test and emitter check
